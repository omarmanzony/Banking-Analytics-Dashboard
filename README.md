<h2>Project Overview</h2>

Banking Analytics Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze banking operations, customer activity, account balances, card usage, transactions, and loan portfolios.

The dashboard provides a structured analytical view of customer acquisition, account distribution, transaction activity, loan performance, card expiration trends, and customer support operations through interactive visualizations and filters.

<h2>Business Objectives</h2>

- Analyze overall banking performance and key business metrics.
- Understand customer distribution and acquisition trends.
- Evaluate account balances across different account types.
- Analyze card distribution and card issuance patterns.
- Monitor card expiration trends across different years.
- Analyze transaction amounts by month and transaction type.
- Evaluate loan amounts and loan distribution by type.
- Compare average interest rates across loan types.
- Monitor customer support calls and resolution status.
- Identify the most common customer support issues.
- Provide an interactive dashboard to support data-driven banking decisions.

<h2>Key Performance Indicators</h2>

The dashboard provides a set of core banking performance metrics, including:

- Total Customers
- Total Accounts
- Total Transaction Amount
- Total Loan Amount
- Customers with Loans
- Customers with Cards
- Total Support Calls
- Total Balance
- Total Cards
- Average Account Balance
- Average Transaction Amount
- Total Loans
- Average Interest Rate

These KPIs provide a high-level overview of customer activity, account performance, transactions, lending operations, card usage, and customer support.

<h2>Data Modeling</h2>

The project uses a relational data model connecting customer, account, transaction, loan, card, and customer support data to support integrated analysis across the dashboard.

The data model includes:

- Transactions
- Customers
- Accounts
- Loans
- Cards
- SupportCalls
- _Measure

The model establishes relationships between the relevant tables to support analysis of customer activity, account performance, transactions, loans, cards, and support calls. The _Measure table organizes analytical measures used throughout the dashboard.
